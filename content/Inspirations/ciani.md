Suzanne Ciani is an electronic music legend. She has done so much work for synthesis in genera. Originally a student of [Don Buchla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Buchla), she came into her own by performing on the Buchla synthesizer. 

This is her [basic performance patch](https://web.uvic.ca/~aschloss/course_mat/MU307/MU307%20Labs/Lab3_BUCHLA/Suzanne%20Ciani/Ciani_BuchlaCookbook.pdf). That document has really influenced how I approach patching a modular synthesizer. I will write up some patch notes one of these days.
