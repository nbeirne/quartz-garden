# Spoiler Free Toronto

This page is meant to indicate locations which I personally enjoy. It's a mix of the best places, my favourites, and the like.

## Cheap or Free Nightlife

### Music

- [The Transac](https://tranzac.org)
- [Drom Taberna](https://www.dromtaberna.com)
- [Houndstooth](https://www.instagram.com/houndstoothto/?hl=en)
- [Handlebar](https://thehandlebar.ca)

### Recurring Events

- [Exit Points (last friday)](https://www.arraymusic.ca/exit-points/)
- [Toronto Electronic Music Open Mic (second monday)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1054052445215240/)
- [Bike Party](https://www.instagram.com/bikepartytoronto/)
- [Bike Rave](https://linktr.ee/bikerave)
- [Critical Mass](https://www.criticalmass.com/toronto)
- [ROM After Dark](https://www.rom.on.ca/whats-on/special-programs/rom-after-dark)
- Vinyl Night @ Communist's Daughter (every monday)
- Render File @ Wenona (every wednesday)
- Coffee & Code (tuesdays @ The Black Pony)
- Drag Me to the Movies @ Paradise Theater (second friday)
- Doors Open Toronto (yearly in May)
- Rocky Horror Picture Show (by Excited Mental State, usually the last Friday of the month)
