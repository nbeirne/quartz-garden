
This is the list of valid transit lines. Generally we include:
- Subways (lines 1-6)
- Street Cars (All 500 level lines)
- Express busses (900 lines, see below)
- GO transit lines (see below)
- UP Express

## Busses
We only accept _express bus routes_. Slower speed busses may be used, however _only express stops count as stops_.

https://www.ttc.ca/express-network
