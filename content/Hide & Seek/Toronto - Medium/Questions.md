There are five categories of questions available to seekers:

1. Matching Questions
2. Measuring questions
3. Radar Questions
4. Thermometer Questions
5. Photo Questions

In general, if the locations or "things" is not in the boundary of the map, it does not exist for the purpose of this question. Also see the rulebook for detailed rules about what counts - but in general it is up to the seekers to clarify any details.

## Matching Questions

"Is your closest \_\_\_\_ the same as my closest \_\_\_\_"?

Valid answers are "yes" or "no". 

Hiders draw 3 and keep 1. 

Fill in the blank with:
- [[Commercial Airport]]
- [[Transit Line]] (yes if the seekers would stop at the hider's station)
- [[Station Name]]'s Length
- Street or Path
- [[Toronto Wards]]
- [[Toronto Community Council Areas]]
- [[Park]]
- [[Zoo]]
- [[Aquarium]]
- [[Golf Course]]
- [[Museum]]
- [[Movie Theater]]
- [[Hospital]]
- [[Library]]
- [[Foreign Consulate]]

## Radars

"Are you within \_\_\_\_ of me?" 

Valid answers are "yes" or "no". 

Use the google maps measuring tool for this please. 

Hiders draw 2 and keep 1. 

Valid distances:
- 0.5km
- 1km
- 1.5km
- 2.5km
- 5km
- 8km
- 16km
- 40km
- 80km
- Choose (any distance you want)

## Thermometer Questions

"After traveling \_\_\_\_, and I hotter or colder?" 

Valid answers are "hotter" or "colder". 

Hiders draw 2 and keep 1. 

Valid distances:
- 1km
- 2.5km
- 5km
- 16km

## Photo questions

"Send me a photo of \_\_\_\_". 

Valid answers are a photo, posted to group chat, which meets the full specifications of the photo question. 

Hiders will have 10 minutes to answer this question. Hiders draw 1 and keep 1. 

Fill in the blank with:
- Any building visible from the transition station
- Widest Street in your hiding zone
- Tree
- Tallest structure in your current sightline
- You
- The Sky
- Tallest building visible from the train station
- Trace nearest street/path
- 2 Buildings
- Restaurant Interior
- Park
- Grocery Store Aisle
- Place of Worship
- Train Platform

## Tentacle Questions

"Within \_\_\_\_ km of me, which \_\_\_\_ are you nearest to? (You must also be within \_\_\_\_)". 

Hiders must be within reach of the tentacle. The answer to this question is a location (use the exact term provided in the lists)

Valid answers are a photo, posted to group chat, which meets the full specifications of the photo question. 

Hiders will have 5 minutes to answer this question. Hiders draw 4 and keep 2. 

Valid locations are:
- [[Museum]]s within 2km.
- [[Library]] within 2km.
- [[Movie Theater]] within 2km.
- [[Hospital]] within 2km.
