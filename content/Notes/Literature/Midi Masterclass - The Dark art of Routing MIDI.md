Midi in: 
- clocks
- instruments use this to receive commands
- controllers _may_ have this for clocks, of chord features.
- should be configurable, but probably does not change.
Midi thru:
- Pass through midi in without changing it. Useful for chaining.
Midi out:
- Controllers use this. Should be configurable on the controller itself.
- An instrument may also have midi out.

Most studios probably want a variety of instruments and controllers. the controllers should be able to play any instruments.
