## Her Approach

Her basic approach is to focus on two modules:
- ER-101
- Verbose Harmonic Oscillator

She practices "minimalism" in sequences. Even getting to the point of having a single sequence, and modifying it (she is quoted as saying there are 100 variations she can make from a single sequence)
- gate patterns??
- attenuate the sequence
- sequence speed without affecting gate speed???

Generally her setup consists of:
- ER-101
- Verbose Harmonic Oscillator
- Random Source (sometimes wogglebug, sometimes frap tools)
- Echophon
- Erbe-Verb
- Maths (or other DUSG)
- Complex oscillator (DPO usually, sometimes Bresno)
- Doepher ADSR
- Doepher VCFQ
- Deopher quad vca/mixer

Very little in the way of modulation.
